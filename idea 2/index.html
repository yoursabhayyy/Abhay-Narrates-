02 / PHILOSOPHY

THE EDIT

"Editing is where story, rhythm and emotion meet."

STORY

01
Finding the narrative inside the footage.

Every sequence must serve an emotional or conceptual purpose. Editing is not assembling fragments—it is excavating the underlying truth of the scene and eliminating everything that dilutes it.

RHYTHM

02
Knowing when a shot should stay — and when it should move.

Pacing is musical. The duration of a cut dictates breathing room, tension, and release. True rhythm comes from listening to the internal heartbeat of a performance.

EMOTION

03
Using timing, sound and image to shape how a moment feels.

A cut is an emotional contract with the viewer. By interweaving precise image selection with deliberate sound design, editorial timing shapes empathy.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

04
Building continuity and meaning through images.

Angles, eyelines, spatial geography, and lens choice speak a silent grammar. An editor orchestrates these cues so the visual progression feels instinctive.
